Product session

One approval session, seen from both sides.

These captures come from the current Cockpit Design direction. Choose a moment to see the operator’s returned batch, the client’s review page, and the bounded handoff back to Meta. They are design proposals, not shipped product screenshots.

Operator · returned batch

See the client’s answer against every creative.

The returned batch keeps each yes, no, and comment beside the exact asset that received it—along with the production record and open findings.

Four approved. One rejected with a comment. No reply has to be translated from a chat thread.

Client · focused review

Give the client one page with one job.

The client sees the actual ad, decides yes or no, and can leave a note. They do not need Meta access, and nothing runs when they send the page back.

The review surface stays calm because campaign controls and internal dashboard noise never reach the client.

Operator · bounded handoff

Make the next action obvious—and bounded.

The dashboard keeps the returned decision next to the publish checkpoint. Only approved creatives move forward, Meta write access is explicit, and every ad it creates starts switched off.

The product removes the upload hunt; the operator still owns every spend decision in Meta.
